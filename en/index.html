What we believe

Tools for saying what's hard to say.

Six principles. Not slogans on a wall: each one is backed by real decisions inside the apps.

01

Privacy is the mechanic, not the fine print.

In Sync nobody sees your swipes: only what both marked is revealed. In Orbit, whoever tracks their cycle rules field by field, revocable instantly — and privacy is never premium. Spark plays without an account, offline.

02

One-time payment, no subscription.

One payment — no subscription is, word for word, the headline of our purchase screen. You pay once, and nothing that makes the product what it is sits behind it: seeing a match, reading it and talking it over in Sync is always free.

03

No tricks.

No punitive streaks, no come back tomorrow, no invented numbers, no manipulative celebrations. Sync's teasers are always real; Orbit never claims she will feel X.

04

Built to put the phone down.

Spark gives each question one minute because the app wins when you put the phone down. Sync turns every match into a dated plan and an entry in the Our story journal. Orbit turns a phase into an action for today.

05

Suggestive, never explicit.

Everything intimate is written so it can be read aloud in a bar. Zero anatomy, zero vulgarity, zero corniness.

06

One design house.

All four share geometry — softened, never round —, dark theme by default and a single hue per app, with green reserved for status. Bilingual ES/EN; launching in Ireland and Mexico.
